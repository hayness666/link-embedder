Privacy Policy

Link Embedder processes new Discord messages to create link previews. It keeps IDs for post controls, but does not save message bodies, usernames, URLs, or media in its own files or database.

1. Data the bot uses

The bot uses message text, message and channel IDs, server IDs, and author and permission information to find supported links, create cards, and limit repeated responses. It does not scan message history. Eligible messages are replaced only after a card is posted and the original is rechecked. Cards show the sender’s display name and avatar. Reply cards link to the referenced message without copying its text.

The bot does not use social-account cookies or bypass private-content, login, age, or anti-bot restrictions. It cannot classify linked media, and age-restricted Discord channels are not automatically excluded.

2. Storage and retention

  • Temporary data. The bot and Discord software keep operational data in memory. Duplicate-message and idle-server records expire after ten minutes, with shorter timers for rate limits. Cleanup happens on later qualifying messages or shutdown, so expired records can remain until then. Facebook author lookups may be cached in memory for up to five minutes. Message caching is disabled.
  • Post-control records. A private file stores message, author, server, channel, parent-channel, webhook, and reply-target IDs, plus an identifier for preserved sender text. It contains no message bodies, media, or tokens. Records survive restarts and remain until managed deletion, rollback, or a verified deletion request. The store holds at most 10,000 records. If a record cannot be saved, the original message stays intact.
  • Logs and hosting. Application logs use fixed status and error messages, without intentionally logging message content, URLs, raw API errors, or credentials. Silly Development hosts the bot and may retain logs and network or operational data under its own practices. No fixed hosting retention period has been verified.
  • Discord messages. Cards remain in Discord until deleted under its policies. Removing the bot does not delete existing cards. Authors can manage tracked cards through the card’s controls or Apps → Manage Post. Older, untracked cards need a moderator. Reply links stop working if their targets are deleted.

3. Preview services and sharing

Preview requests send the relevant public post URL or identifier to the source platform or an approved helper. They do not include surrounding Discord message text, Discord user identity, or credentials. Requests can also reach platform media servers. Discord may fetch media and crawl original links.

The supported-sites table lists the approach for every supported platform. Amazon, Pinterest, Snapchat, and UpScrolled use public platform metadata. Approved helpers serve Bluesky, Facebook, Instagram, Reddit, Threads, TikTok, and Twitter. Helpers are independent services and require operator approval. That approval is not user consent or a claim of a contracted-processor relationship or legal compliance.

Specific media routes include Pinterest’s i.pinimg.com, Snapchat’s bolt-gcdn.sc-cdn.net and cf-st.sc-cdn.net, and Bluesky’s pds-cache.fxbsky.app. Facebook previews use Facebed and public Facebook metadata and media servers. Facebook author lookups may supply verified handles. For Snapchat and approved Facebook video and Reel attachments, the bot downloads validated public media into memory, within a shared 8 MiB limit, and sends it to Discord without saving the files.

Platforms, helpers, and media services may record requests under their own policies. Known tracking parameters are removed, but functional share parameters can remain. This does not guarantee zero tracking. Unavailable or blocked content is not bypassed. Without helper approval, previews are limited to available public first-party metadata.

4. Your controls and requests

Wrap links in angle brackets, spoiler markup, or code to suppress previews. Administrators can restrict channel access or remove the bot. Use the contact form to request access, correction, or deletion of operator-held data, or report a privacy concern. Include a reply address if you need follow-up. We may need non-sensitive details to locate a record and verify the request.

The operator cannot erase records held independently by Discord or other services. Contact those services separately. Do not put sensitive information in public reports.

5. Website and contact form

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Apart from the Google Forms contact form, it has no analytics scripts, external fonts, or advertising. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security, including signed-out visitors. See GitHub Pages data collection and GitHub’s Privacy Statement.

Loading the contact form connects your browser to Google, which may process IP addresses, browser information, cookies, and other service data under Google’s Privacy Policy. Submitting sends your category, message, optional reply email, and submission time to Google Forms for the operator to review. The form does not require sign-in, accept file uploads, or automatically collect a verified email. Response summaries are not public.

Form responses remain in the operator’s Google Forms account until deleted, with no automatic retention deadline. The owner receives new-response email notifications. Support emails and follow-up records may remain in the operator’s mailbox until deleted. You can request deletion through the form. Do not submit passwords, tokens, private URLs, medical information, or identity documents.
