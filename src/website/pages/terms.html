The bot operates where channel permissions allow. It does not require Administrator, change permissions, or force access. Administrators can restrict it or remove it.

Authors can manage tracked cards through the card’s controls or Apps → Manage Post. Older, untracked posts need a moderator. Removing the bot stops future activity but does not erase existing messages. Ask an administrator to remove a response.
